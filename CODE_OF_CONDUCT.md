# 📜 Code of Conduct

## 🤝 Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for everyone.

---

## ✅ Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and kind
- Use welcoming and inclusive language
- Accept constructive feedback
- Focus on what is best for the community

---

## ❌ Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or discrimination
- Offensive comments or personal attacks
- Trolling or insulting behavior

---

## ⚖️ Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior.

They may take appropriate action in response to unacceptable behavior.

---

## 📬 Contact

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it by opening an issue.

---

## ❤️ Final Note

Let’s build something great together in a respectful and supportive environment.
